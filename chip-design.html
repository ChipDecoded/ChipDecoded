Engineers first define what the chip should do, what features it needs and what constraints it must meet.

💻 Front-End vs Back-End

Chip design is often discussed in two broad areas.

🧠

Front-End Design

Focuses mainly on the chip's functionality and digital logic before the physical layout is created.

Specification & Architecture
RTL Design
Functional Verification

📐

Back-End Design

Focuses on turning the logical design into a physical layout that can be manufactured.

🧩 Different Areas of Chip Design

Different engineering disciplines work together to create a complete chip.

0️⃣1️⃣

Digital Design

Works with digital logic, registers, processors, controllers and other logic-based circuits.

〰️

Analog Design

Deals with continuously varying electrical signals, such as those found in amplifiers and sensor circuits.

🔀

Mixed-Signal Design

Combines digital and analog circuits in systems where both types of signals are needed.

📐

Physical Design

Converts logical structures into a physical chip layout containing cells, wires and power structures.

🧪

Verification

Checks whether the design behaves according to its intended specifications.

🛠️

DFT

Design-for-Test techniques help make manufactured chips easier to test for defects.

🏗️ Inside a Chip Architecture

A processor or complex SoC can contain many functional blocks.

CPU Core Executes instructions.
Cache Keeps frequently needed data close to processing logic.
Memory Controller Manages communication with memory.
GPU / Accelerator Handles specialized workloads.
Interconnect Connects different blocks.
I/O Connects the chip with external interfaces.
