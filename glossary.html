Semiconductor Glossary

Simple explanations of important terms used in chip design, semiconductors, VLSI and modern electronics.

Why a Semiconductor Glossary?

Semiconductor technology contains many technical terms that can be difficult for beginners to understand.

This glossary provides simple explanations of commonly used terms related to chips, transistors, chip design, manufacturing and packaging.

A

ASIC

Application-Specific Integrated Circuit. A chip designed for a particular application or purpose.

AI Accelerator

Specialized hardware designed to speed up selected artificial intelligence workloads.

Analog Circuit

A circuit that processes continuously varying electrical signals rather than only discrete digital states.

C

CMOS

Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor. A widely used technology for building digital integrated circuits.

CPU

Central Processing Unit. A general-purpose processor that executes instructions and performs many types of computing tasks.

Chiplet

A smaller semiconductor die designed to work as part of a larger multi-die system.

Cleanroom

A controlled manufacturing environment designed to reduce contamination during semiconductor processing.

D

Deposition

A manufacturing process used to form a thin layer of material on a wafer.

Die

A small piece of semiconductor material containing an integrated circuit.

Doping

The controlled introduction of impurities into semiconductor material to modify its electrical properties.

DSP

Digital Signal Processor. A processor designed to efficiently handle many types of digital signal processing operations.

F

Fab

Short for fabrication facility, a specialized factory where semiconductor wafers are processed.

FinFET

A transistor structure in which the channel forms a raised fin, allowing the gate to control the channel from multiple sides.

FPGA

Field-Programmable Gate Array. A programmable semiconductor device whose hardware logic can be configured after manufacturing.

G

GPU

Graphics Processing Unit. A processor designed for highly parallel computation and commonly used for graphics and AI workloads.

GAA

Gate-All-Around. A transistor structure in which the gate surrounds the channel, providing strong electrostatic control.

I

Integrated Circuit

An electronic circuit containing multiple components formed together on a semiconductor die.

Interconnect

A conductive connection used to carry electrical signals between components or circuit blocks.

Ion Implantation

A semiconductor manufacturing technique used to introduce selected ions into a material to modify its properties.

L

Lithography

A patterning process used to define selected features on a semiconductor wafer.

Logic Gate

A digital circuit element that performs a logical operation such as AND, OR or NOT.

M

Microcontroller

A compact integrated circuit containing a processor, memory and peripherals for controlling electronic systems.

Memory

Semiconductor hardware used to store data or instructions.

MOSFET

Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor. A widely used transistor type in integrated circuits.

N

NPU

Neural Processing Unit. Specialized hardware designed to accelerate certain neural-network operations.

Node

A term used to describe a semiconductor manufacturing technology generation. Node names do not directly represent one single physical dimension of a chip.

P

Photolithography

A process that uses light and photoresist to transfer patterns onto a semiconductor wafer.

Physical Design

The stage of chip design where circuit structures are placed and connected physically before manufacturing.

Photoresist

A light-sensitive material used during semiconductor patterning processes.

R

RTL

Register Transfer Level. A way of describing digital hardware behavior and data movement between registers.

Routing

The process of creating physical connections between placed circuit elements during chip physical design.

S

SoC

System on Chip. An integrated circuit that combines multiple system functions on one semiconductor platform.

Synthesis

A design process that converts an RTL description into a hardware representation suitable for implementation.

Semiconductor

A material whose electrical properties can be controlled and modified, making it useful for electronic devices.

Silicon Wafer

A thin, flat piece of semiconductor material used as a foundation for manufacturing integrated circuits.

T

Transistor

A semiconductor device that can control electrical current and is a fundamental building block of modern electronic circuits.

TSMC

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, a major semiconductor foundry that manufactures chips designed by other companies.

V

VLSI

Very Large-Scale Integration. The integration of a very large number of electronic components into an integrated circuit.

Verification

The process of checking whether a chip design behaves according to its intended specifications.

W

Wafer

A thin semiconductor disk on which many integrated circuits can be manufactured.

Wafer Testing

Electrical testing performed on individual die locations while they are still part of the wafer.

Quick Concept Map

Transistor → building block of digital circuits

Logic Gates → built using transistors

Digital Circuits → combined to create larger hardware blocks

VLSI → integrates very large numbers of components

Chip → complete integrated circuit used in an electronic system

Note:

Semiconductor terminology can have more detailed definitions depending on the context. The explanations on this page are simplified for learning and quick reference.
