Types of Chips

Modern electronic devices use many different kinds of semiconductor chips. Each type is designed to perform particular tasks such as computation, memory, communication, control or signal processing.

What Is a Chip?

A semiconductor chip, also called an integrated circuit (IC), is a small piece of semiconductor material that contains electronic components and their connections.

Depending on its design, a chip can perform calculations, store information, control electronic systems, process signals or communicate with other devices.

Major Types of Chips

CPU

CPU

A Central Processing Unit executes instructions and performs general-purpose computing tasks. CPUs are commonly used in computers, servers and many other electronic systems.

GPU

GPU

A Graphics Processing Unit is designed to perform many calculations in parallel. GPUs are widely used for graphics, scientific computing and machine learning.

MCU

Microcontroller

A microcontroller combines a processor, memory and peripheral interfaces on one chip. It is commonly used in embedded systems and control applications.

FPGA

FPGA

A Field-Programmable Gate Array contains configurable logic resources that can be programmed for different digital hardware applications.
